---
title: The four results
description: UPHELD, REJECTED, CORRECTED and STANDS, and what each one does with the money.
---

The model answers one label. The **verdict is computed in code from that label**, so what a challenge earns is a rule in the contract and never the model's opinion.

| Verdict | When | Money | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UPHELD** | The label is MISSTATES or UNSUPPORTED | The stake comes back, plus 20% of the original bond to the challenger | The claim is marked on the summary |
| **REJECTED** | The label is FAITHFUL | The stake goes to the author | The claim is marked as tested |
| **CORRECTED** | The author retracts the claim before it is read | The stake comes back, plus 10% of the bond | The claim is struck |
| **STANDS** | The window ends with no open challenge | The rest of the bond returns to the author | A clean record on the summary |

## The labels

- **FAITHFUL**: the claim says what the clause says, in any wording or language, and adds nothing that matters.
- **MISSTATES**: the claim conflicts with a clause, overstates it, or drops a condition that changes the meaning (all instead of some, no notice instead of notice, fully instead of partly).
- **UNSUPPORTED**: the clause does not say what the claim says, and does not contradict it.

## One addition to the spec

A challenge that nobody could get a verdict for within 48 hours is voided by the next `judge` call: no model runs, the stake goes back to the challenger, and the claim stays open to a new challenge. It is a safety valve so that no money waits forever; it decides nothing about the claim. See [Limitations](/docs/more/limitations).
